Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CareerPilot AI ("we", "our", "the service") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • CV/resume files you upload. We read the text from your file to run the AI analysis, cover letter, or interview-question features. Files are processed in memory and are not saved to disk or stored after your request completes.
  • Job descriptions you paste in. Used only to compare against your CV for that request; not stored.
  • Email address. Collected only if you subscribe to Pro (via our payment provider, Paddle) or use the "restore access" feature, so we can confirm and remember your subscription.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider and AI provider may log standard technical information (like IP address and timestamps) for security and reliability, as is normal for any website.

How your data is used

Your CV text and job description are sent to Google's Gemini AI to generate your analysis, cover letter, or interview questions. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your CV content for advertising.

Cookies

If you subscribe to Pro, we set one small cookie in your browser so the site remembers your subscription and does not apply the free daily limit. This cookie contains only a signed reference to your Pro status — no CV content.

Third parties we use

  • Google Gemini — processes your CV text and job description to generate results.
  • Paddle — processes payments for Pro subscriptions and acts as merchant of record.
  • Supabase and Render — power our database and hosting.

Your choices

You can use the Free plan without ever providing an email address. If you subscribe, you can cancel anytime through the link in your payment receipt, or by contacting us.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at the email address shown on our Terms of Service page.
